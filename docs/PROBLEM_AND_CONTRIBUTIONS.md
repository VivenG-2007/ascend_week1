# Problem statement and team contributions

## Problem
Students, parents and visitors cannot easily find official college information. It lives in PDF circulars, photographed notice boards, timetables and individual people's heads, and much of it is needed in Telugu or by voice. People queue at offices or rely on rumours and outdated circulars.

## Solution
CampusVoice answers questions by text or voice in English and Telugu, **only** from official uploaded documents and structured college data, citing the source, its date and the page. When a newer circular replaces an older one it uses the newer one and says so. When it cannot answer it says so and raises a ticket with the right department; it declines off-topic questions and never handles personal student data.
